# Reference review — September 27, 2026

Reviewed in a local Chromium browser:

- https://www.silviamalavasi.com/ — staged reveal, decisive typography, motion linked to an explicit narrative. Its medical/dissection concept and artwork were not reused.
- https://daqconsulting.com/ — restrained dark environment, large hierarchy, architectural explanation with inspectable stages. No assets or layout copied.
- https://launchfar.com/ — immersive opening, strong material/typography contrast, concrete product interfaces further down the page. Its portrait/particle art was not reused.
- https://arc.net/folder/067E6535-20F7-47B4-A130-262501672322 — a collection of design directories including Layers, Saaspo, Godly, Httpster, Minimal Gallery, Lapa Ninja, Siteinspire, Landbook, and others. The collection itself was reviewed; this is not a claim that every linked directory was exhaustively reviewed.
- https://medium.com/design-bootcamp/10-of-the-coolest-websites-for-inspiration-d8b2b35bbc2 — the page reader returned only site navigation; Chromium received a Cloudflare blocked page. The article content could not be reviewed.

The resulting portfolio opening follows the user's explicit story: start with recognisable ideas, connect the systems and AI, and arrive at a usable product. It is a reversible scroll timeline with an interactive Buddy study, not an abstract sculpture or floating stack of cards. The scene is built from native text, SVG paths, and CSS perspective, preserving accessibility and product interaction.
